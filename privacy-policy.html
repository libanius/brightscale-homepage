Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This page is in English. Spanish and Portuguese versions are available on request.

BrightScale Group LLC, doing business as BrightScale Remodeling ("BrightScale", "we", "us"), plans and manages home remodeling and repair projects in Broward and Palm Beach counties, Florida. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, phone number, email, ZIP code or address, project details, photos you send, and appointment preferences, through our forms, phone calls, text messages, email, or referral partners such as The Home Depot Pro Referral program when you request a service there.
  • Calls with our virtual assistant: when you call 954-553-9535, our AI virtual assistant (Brighty) answers. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so our team can follow up on your request.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store card numbers.
  • Website data: basic technical data (browser, pages visited). Analytics and advertising cookies (Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel) load only if you choose "Allow" on our cookie banner.

How we use information

  • To answer your request, schedule visits, prepare proposals and deliver your project.
  • To send you call summaries, appointment confirmations, project updates, receipts and payment links by email or text message (see Text messaging below).
  • To keep records required for our business, taxes and warranties.
  • To improve our website and services.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Licensed contractors and crew members working on your project, limited to what they need to do the work.
  • Service providers that operate our business tools (for example: phone and SMS delivery, email, scheduling, payment processing, website hosting), bound to use it only for those services.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and check the SMS consent box on our website, or ask for text updates on a call with our team or our virtual assistant, BrightScale Remodeling (BrightScale Group LLC) may send you text messages about your request: replies to your questions, appointment scheduling and reminders, call summaries, proposals, payment links and project updates. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

  • Frequency: varies with your project, usually a few messages per request.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Help: reply HELP, call 954-553-9535 or email contact@brightscalegroup.com.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver the messages (for example, our SMS platform), and only for that purpose.

Data retention and security

We keep project records for as long as needed for the project, warranties and legal obligations, and protect them with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop texts.
  • Decline cookies on our banner, or clear them in your browser.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing contact@brightscalegroup.com.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
