## Not stock — but still placeholders

`hero-*.jpg` on the service pages are the original AI-generated renders, re-encoded from
PNG to JPEG. The generator's sparkle watermark that was baked into the bottom-right corner
of seven of them has been cropped out, and the set dropped from ~12 MB to ~1 MB. They are
on-context, so they were kept rather than swapped — but they still read as renders, not
photographs, and should be replaced by real job-site photography.

`hero-pro.png` → `hero-pro.jpg` is 639×583, which is soft on a retina screen at its
display size. It needs a real BrightScale team photo at 1600px or wider.
