Program name: BrightScale Remodeling customer care and appointment messages.

Sender: BrightScale Group LLC, doing business as BrightScale Remodeling.

How you opt in

You opt in in one of these ways:

  • Referral request: you submit a service request on The Home Depot Pro Referral platform and agree to be contacted by the assigned service provider. Our first text identifies BrightScale Remodeling, references your request and explains how to opt out.
  • Phone call: you call our business line (954-553-9535) and, when our virtual assistant Brighty asks whether we may text you a call summary and a link to confirm your visit, you say yes. Your answer is recorded in the call transcript.
  • Website: you check the optional SMS consent box on our visit request form.
  • Texting us first: you send a text to our business number.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and check the SMS consent box on our website, or ask for text updates on a call with our team or our virtual assistant, BrightScale Remodeling (BrightScale Group LLC) may send you text messages about your request: replies to your questions, appointment scheduling and reminders, call summaries, proposals, payment links and project updates. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

  • Frequency: varies with your project, usually a few messages per request.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Help: reply HELP, call 954-553-9535 or email contact@brightscalegroup.com.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver the messages (for example, our SMS platform), and only for that purpose.

Sample messages

BrightScale Remodeling: Hi Anthony, we received your service request through The Home Depot Pro Referral. We can help. Reply CALL and our assistant Brighty will call you, or reply here with a good time to talk. Reply STOP to opt out.

BrightScale Remodeling: Thanks for calling. Proposed visit: Sat, Oct 18 at 10 AM. Reply YES to confirm or reply with another time. Reply STOP to opt out.

BrightScale Remodeling: Your visit is confirmed for Sat, Oct 18 at 10 AM. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Keywords

  • CALL: our virtual assistant Brighty calls you back to schedule.
  • YES: confirms the proposed visit time.
  • HELP: returns our contact details. STOP: opts you out. START: opts you back in.
